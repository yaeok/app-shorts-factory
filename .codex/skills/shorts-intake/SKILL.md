# Shorts Intake

Produce a usable `workspace.json` and an explicit creative direction. Inspect supplied repositories, store materials, screenshots, and existing channel evidence before asking for facts already available.

Resolve product truth, primary audience, user problem, promise, proof, conversion KPI, brand assets, prohibited claims, channel constraints, cadence, approval mode, content pillars, and the first controlled hypothesis.

Decide whether the operation needs a finite product-introduction sequence, a repeatable series, or both. Read [references/interview.md](references/interview.md) when conducting a live discovery conversation.

Do not invent ratings, download counts, testimonials, prices, availability, or features. Mark unknown facts as unresolved. End with a concise brief, asset inventory, risks, and the recommended first recipe.
