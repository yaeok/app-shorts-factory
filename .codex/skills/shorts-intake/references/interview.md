# Direction interview

Ask only for missing decisions, one compact question at a time when input is required:

1. Outcome: which user action matters after viewing?
2. Audience: who feels the problem most and in what moment?
3. Truth: which features or outcomes are strongest and verifiable?
4. Creative: calm, playful, expert, competitive, emotional, or another tone?
5. Series: what can be generated repeatedly without becoming misleading or stale?
6. Operations: channels, cadence, approval mode, and metric access.

Recommend a direction instead of returning an unranked list. Record rejected directions and why.
